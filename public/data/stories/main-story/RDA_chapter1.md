Noah Fischer doesn't get jealous. He especially doesn't get jealous over Yu Yu-won, the marketing department's resident chaos agent who somehow always manages to disrupt Noah's carefully ordered world. And he absolutely, definitely isn't counting the number of times Yoo Jaehyun has "accidentally" touched Yu-won in the past hour.

Except he is. Seven. Seven unnecessary touches, from the casual hand on Yu-won's shoulder as Jaehyun leans in to look at his phone, to the way he keeps ruffling Yu-won's hair like he has any right to do that. Not that Noah's keeping track.

From his position at the cafe's counter, Noah takes another sip of his rapidly cooling americano, pretending he's not watching their little... meeting. Because that's what this is supposed to be - a simple discussion about the upcoming marketing campaign. So why does Jaehyun need to sit so close?

"Hyung, look at this one!" Yu-won's excited voice carries across the cafe, and Noah's grip on his cup tightens imperceptibly. Since when does Yu-won call Jaehyun 'hyung'?

"Cute," Jaehyun laughs, and his hand is back on Yu-won's shoulder. Eight touches. "You always find the best references, Yu-won-ah."

The casual familiarity makes something dark twist in Noah's chest. He's known Yu-won longer, worked with him closer, and yet he's never allowed himself that kind of casual intimacy. Always keeping his distance, maintaining that professional wall. Now, watching someone else so easily cross those boundaries...

"Fischer-ssi?" One of the interns appears at his elbow, startling him from his thoughts. "The 3PM meeting..."

"Cancel it," Noah says shortly, not taking his eyes off the scene across the cafe. Yu-won is showing Jaehyun something on his phone again, their heads bent close together. Nine touches, as Jaehyun's hand finds Yu-won's wrist.

Later, when he catches Yu-won alone in the break room, Noah can't stop himself from commenting. His voice is carefully neutral as he makes his coffee, but his grip on the mug betrays his tension.

"You really don't notice, do you?"

"Notice what?" Yu-won looks up from his phone, all innocent eyes and confused pout. It's infuriating how he can look so oblivious.

"The way Jaehyun was all over you." Noah doesn't look at him, focusing way too intently on stirring his coffee. "He's not exactly subtle with the touching."

"Eh? Hyung's just friendly-" Yu-won starts, then freezes when Noah's head snaps up at the word 'hyung'.

"Oh? He's 'hyung' now?" Noah's smile is sharp enough to cut. "Interesting."

Yu-won tilts his head, and something shifts in his expression - a flash of... understanding? Mischief? "Why? Does it bother you?"

"Why would it bother me?" Noah's voice is too controlled, too even. "I'm simply pointing out that some people might get the wrong idea."

"What kind of wrong idea?" Yu-won steps closer, and suddenly the break room feels much smaller. "That I might like him?"

Noah's coffee cup hits the counter with more force than necessary. "Do you?"

"Noah-ssi..." Yu-won's voice has gone soft, teasing. "Are you jealous?"

"Don't be ridiculous." But Noah doesn't step back when Yu-won moves even closer. "I'm just concerned about workplace propriety."

"Mmhm." Yu-won's smile grows. "So if I told you Jaehyun-hyung asked me to dinner..."

The temperature in the room seems to drop several degrees. Noah's eyes darken as he finally meets Yu-won's gaze. "Did he?"

"Would it matter if he did?"

They're standing too close now, the air between them charged with something neither of them is ready to name. Noah opens his mouth to reply - to deny, to deflect, to say anything that might restore his carefully maintained control - when the break room door opens.

"Yu-won-ah!" Jaehyun's voice breaks the moment. "Ready for lunch?"

Noah steps back so quickly he almost knocks over his coffee. Yu-won's eyes linger on him for a moment longer, that knowing smile still playing at his lips, before he turns to Jaehyun.

"Coming, hyung!"

As Yu-won follows Jaehyun out, he glances back over his shoulder. The look he gives Noah is pure challenge - like he's finally figured out exactly how to crack that perfect control.

Noah watches them go, his forgotten coffee growing cold on the counter. He doesn't get jealous, he reminds himself. He especially doesn't get jealous over Yu Yu-won.

But as he returns to his office, he can't help but pull up the company directory on his computer. Maybe it's time to reassign some of Marketing's projects to different account managers. For efficiency's sake, of course. Not because he counted ten touches by the time Yu-won left the cafe.

Definitely not because of that.
