# Rules Don't Apply
## Chapter 3: The Night Everything Changed

### The First Kiss

Those three words hang in the air between them. Yu-won's fingers tremble where they're clutched in Noah's shirt, his heart hammering so hard he's sure Noah must feel it. He's never been this brave - or this terrified.

Noah stares down at him, something dark and unreadable in his eyes. In the dim light of his apartment, Yu-won looks softer than Noah's ever seen him - all his sharp edges blurred by alcohol and honesty. It would be so easy to believe this means something.

But then Noah remembers how naturally Yu-won fits against Jaehyun, how easily he accepts those casual touches. This probably means nothing - just drunk neediness that Yu-won shares with everyone who gets close enough.

Still, when their lips finally meet, Yu-won makes this small, broken sound that drives Noah crazy. His kisses are clumsy, eager in a way that feels almost innocent - but that can't be right. Not with how bold Yu-won usually is, how comfortable he seems with intimacy.

"H-hyung," Yu-won gasps between kisses, trying to get closer. He wants to say Noah's name, but even drunk he's too shy, too aware of their professional distance. Maybe if he shows Noah how much he wants this...

But Noah goes rigid at the word, his hands tightening on Yu-won's hips. Of course. Even now, Yu-won's thinking of someone else.

"Show me," Noah growls against his lips, jealousy making him cruel, "what he taught you."

Yu-won's confusion turns to hurt at Noah's tone, but then those hands are everywhere and he can't think straight. Everything feels too much, too intense. He should say something - tell Noah this is all new, that he's scared - but Noah seems so certain that Yu-won knows what he's doing. And isn't this what Noah wants? Someone experienced, someone who won't complicate things with feelings?

So when Noah pulls him toward the bedroom, Yu-won swallows his fear and lets himself be led. Better to pretend than admit the truth - that he's been saving himself for someone who clearly doesn't want his heart.

### The Bedroom

They fall onto silk sheets, Yu-won's head spinning from more than just alcohol. Noah's mouth trails down his neck, each touch making him tremble. It's too much, too intense, but he's terrified of showing how inexperienced he really is.

"Wait-" Yu-won gasps as Noah's hands slide lower. "I've never-"

But Noah's already tugging at his clothes, misreading his hesitation as teasing. "Don't play innocent now. Not after how you act with him."

Yu-won wants to protest - he doesn't understand why Noah sounds so bitter, why he's being so rough. But then Noah's kissing that spot behind his ear and coherent thought becomes impossible.

"Hyung, please..." The word slips out in a desperate whimper as Yu-won arches up. He means it for Noah, wants to be closer, more intimate, but-

Noah freezes. His grip turns bruising. "Don't."

"W-what?"

"Don't say that when you're thinking of him." Noah's voice is ice even as his touch burns. "If you want Jaehyun so badly, why are you here?"

Yu-won's confusion turns to hurt. Why does Noah keep bringing up Jaehyun? Why is he acting like this - like he hates Yu-won even while touching him like he can't get enough?

But before Yu-won can find his voice, Noah's kissing him again, hard and desperate, turning all his questions into breathless sounds. And Yu-won... Yu-won lets himself drown in it.

He'll hate himself tomorrow, they both will, but right now...

### The Morning After

Morning light creeps across expensive sheets, painting everything in soft gold. Yu-won makes a small sound in his sleep, unconsciously seeking warmth as he burrows closer to Noah's chest. His face is peaceful, all traces of last night's desperation gone.

Noah watches him, something dangerous and soft unfurling in his chest. Like this, with his guard completely down, Yu-won looks almost pure. Almost untouched. It's a lie, of course - Noah has the memories of last night to prove otherwise - but for just a moment, he lets himself pretend.

"Annoying," he murmurs, more fond than he means to be, fingers carding through Yu-won's messy hair. Just for these few precious seconds, he can imagine this means something. That Yu-won is actually his.

The touch stirs Yu-won from sleep, consciousness creeping in along with his hangover. That word - 'annoying' - filters through first, followed by the crushing weight of realization. Where he is. What they did. How much it hurts.

His entire body aches in ways that prove exactly how inexperienced he was, but Noah had been so rough, so certain... and now he's calling Yu-won annoying, probably regretting everything...

"This isn't happening," Yu-won whispers, voice cracking as he jerks away. The sudden movement makes his head spin, makes everything below his waist protest, but panic overwhelms the pain. "This can't be happening-"

Noah's soft expression hardens instantly, that rare vulnerability vanishing. "Regretting it already?"

"I-" Yu-won clutches the sheets to his chest like armor, fragments of memory flashing through his mind. Begging for kisses. Noah's hands everywhere. That cruel tone when Yu-won called him 'hyung'... "You must have... you took-"

"Took advantage?" Noah's laugh is sharp enough to draw blood. "Is that what you're going to say? That I forced you?" He leans closer, voice dropping dangerously. "Should we count how many times you begged me not to stop?"

Yu-won flinches, tears pricking at his eyes. He'd wanted it - god, he'd wanted it so much - but not like this. Not with Noah thinking he was experienced, thinking he did this all the time, thinking he wanted-

"Just go back to Jaehyun," Noah continues coldly. "Clearly that's who you wanted anyway."

"What?" Yu-won's head snaps up. "What does he have to do with-"

"You called his name." Each word falls like ice. "Right before I fucked you. Or was that just another game? Testing how many people you can wrap around your finger?"

Horror dawns on Yu-won's face. That 'hyung' had been for Noah - he'd been too shy to use his name, too desperate to think clearly. But Noah thought... all this time, he assumed...

"I didn't-" Yu-won starts, but pride and hurt make his voice sharp instead of honest. "You know what? Fine. Think whatever you want. This was... this was a mistake anyway."

Something dangerous flashes in Noah's eyes. He moves closer, caging Yu-won against the headboard. "A mistake? Funny. You weren't calling it a mistake when you were moaning under me."

"Get away from me!" Yu-won shoves at his chest, panic and hurt making him wild. "I hate this! I hate that it was you!"

Noah goes very still. When he pulls back, his eyes are cold enough to burn. "Get out."

"...you're serious?" Yu-won's voice comes out smaller than he means it to, all his earlier fire crumbling.

"Get. Out." Noah doesn't even look at him, each word precise and cold.

Yu-won swallows hard, pride the only thing keeping him upright. "Gladly!"

He manages to hold it together until he's out the door. But in the empty hallway, everything crashes down at once. He slides to the floor, tears finally spilling over as he presses a hand to his mouth to muffle his sobs.

How did this happen? His first time - the one he'd saved, dreamed about, imagined with the man he'd been crushing on for months - how did it turn into this nightmare?

Neither acknowledges how their hands shake as Yu-won gathers his clothes. How Noah's fingers itch to grab him and make him stay, to demand the truth about that 'hyung', to understand why Yu-won kissed him like it was his first time. How Yu-won's entire body trembles with the effort of holding back tears, of not admitting that he'd wanted this for so long but not like this, never like this.

On one side of the door, Yu-won breaks apart, his first time turning into his worst nightmare. On the other, Noah stands frozen, telling himself the sounds of those muffled sobs are just another act. That Yu-won does this with everyone. That it meant nothing.

They let the silence between them fill with assumptions. With pride. With pain.

Noah tells himself it's better this way. Yu-won clearly belongs to someone else.
Yu-won tells himself Noah must hate him now. It was probably just drunk revenge for all their office fights.

They're both completely, utterly wrong.
