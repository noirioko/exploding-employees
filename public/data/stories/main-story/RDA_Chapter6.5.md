It started like most bad habits do - gradually, then all at once.

After that first conversation in Noah's office, after laying down rules neither of them really believed in, something shifted. Subtle at first - lingering glances across conference rooms, "accidental" touches while reviewing presentations, late nights that stretched longer than necessary.

They told themselves it was just convenience. Just stress relief. Just two adults being practical about their needs.

(They were lying about that too.)

The first time it happened again, Yu-won was absolutely not trying to get Noah's attention. He definitely wasn't wearing his best fitted shirt, and he absolutely wasn't leaning over Noah's desk more than necessary while reviewing the latest mockups.

"If you look here," Yu-won said, pointing to something on his tablet, very aware of how close he was to Noah. "The client specifically requested-"

"Yu-won-ssi." Noah's voice was tight. "Are you doing this on purpose?"

Yu-won blinked innocently. "Doing what?"

Noah's eyes darkened as they traced over Yu-won's form, lingering on where his shirt pulled just slightly across his shoulders. "You know exactly what."

(This was dangerous territory. They both knew it. But Yu-won had spent months watching Noah from afar, dying for even a hint of interest. Now that he knew what Noah's hands felt like, how his kisses tasted... well, maybe he was pushing his luck. Just a little.)

They blamed what happened next on project deadlines. The Anderson campaign was due in three days, and Yu-won had been staring at the same tagline for hours, frustration building with each revision.

"It's still not right," he muttered, pacing Noah's office like a caged animal. "Something's missing."

Noah watched him from behind his desk, sharp eyes following each movement. "You're overthinking it."

"Easy for you to say! You're not the one who has to-" Yu-won's rant cut off as Noah stood, closing the distance between them in three precise steps.

"Yu-won-ssi." Noah's voice was low, deliberate. "Stop pacing."

Maybe it was the commanding tone. Maybe it was the way Noah's fingers caught his wrist, stilling his nervous movement. Or maybe they'd both just been looking for an excuse.

Either way, the tagline lay forgotten on Noah's desk as they crashed together, months of tension finally breaking.

After that, it became... a thing. Not a relationship - they were very clear about that. Just an arrangement. Just sex. Just a way to work off steam between meetings and deadlines.

Like that morning Yu-won dozed off during the weekly marketing review, his head nodding dangerously close to his laptop. A steaming americano appeared in front of him, making him jolt upright.

"Company policy clearly states no sleeping during work hours," Noah said coolly, already walking away. "Do try to stay conscious, Yu-won-ssi."

Yu-won stared at the coffee - made exactly how he liked it, with an extra shot and just a hint of sweetness. His forced smile probably looked more like a grimace. "...thanks."

(How was this the same man he'd been hopelessly crushing on for months? The one he'd flirted with shamelessly until that cursed drunken night changed everything? And why, despite Noah's cold attitude, did Yu-won's heart still skip when he noticed Noah had remembered his coffee preference?)

The rest of the marketing team exchanged knowing looks. They'd watched Yu-won's obvious pining for months - the way he'd light up whenever Noah entered a room, how he'd work twice as hard on projects just to earn Noah's praise. Now, seeing him accept Noah's coffee with that complicated expression... well, the office gossip mill had plenty to work with.

If Yu-won sometimes caught himself watching Noah during presentations, admiring the sharp line of his jaw or the way his voice commanded attention... well, that was just appreciation for good genetics. And if Noah's hands lingered a bit too long when fixing Yu-won's tie, or if his eyes darkened whenever Jaehyun got too close... that was just professional concern.

Like the day Jaehyun caught Yu-won shivering in the break room, his thin dress shirt no match for the office's aggressive air conditioning.

"I swear they're trying to recreate Antarctica in here," Yu-won muttered, hugging his coffee cup for warmth.

Jaehyun's eyes lit up. "Oh! That reminds me." He disappeared to his office, returning with a shopping bag. "I saw this the other day and thought of you. The color reminded me of your hair."

Yu-won peered into the bag, pulling out a soft cream-colored sweater with subtle blue undertones. "Hyung, you didn't have to..."

"Try it on!" Jaehyun insisted, already helping Yu-won slip it over his shirt. The sweater was perfectly oversized, making Yu-won look softer, younger. "See? Perfect fit!"

Yu-won tugged at the sleeves, a shy smile spreading across his face. "It's so warm... Thank you, hyung. You're always taking care of me."

"Anything for my favorite dongsaeng," Jaehyun grinned, reaching out to ruffle Yu-won's hair. "Speaking of which, you still owe me dinner. You've been rain-checking me for weeks now! Tonight?"

Yu-won hesitated, fingers playing with the hem of his new sweater. He had plans with Noah - not that he could tell anyone that. "I have to finish the Anderson presentation..."

"Tomorrow then," Jaehyun said firmly. "No excuses! You need proper food, not just coffee and convenience store kimbap."

"Okay, okay!" Yu-won laughed, the sound carrying through the break room. "Tomorrow, I promise."

Neither noticed Noah standing in the doorway, his expression darkening as he watched Jaehyun adjust the sweater's collar, fingers lingering just a moment too long. The coffee in his hand had gone cold, forgotten as his eyes fixed on how perfectly the sweater suited Yu-won, how comfortable he looked in Jaehyun's gift.

Later that day, during the project review, Noah's criticism was particularly sharp.

"The entire concept needs to be reworked," he said coldly, not looking up from his laptop. "The tone is too soft. Too... comfortable. This is a luxury brand, Yu-won-ssi. Try to remember that."

Yu-won frowned, tugging unconsciously at his sweater sleeves. "But the focus group data shows-"

"The focus groups don't write your paycheck. Fix it."

That night, when Yu-won showed up at Noah's apartment still wearing the sweater, Noah's hands were rougher than usual as he pulled it off.

"I liked that sweater," Yu-won protested breathlessly between kisses.

Noah's only response was to bite down harder on Yu-won's neck, leaving marks that would definitely break rule number two.

(If Yu-won noticed how the sweater mysteriously disappeared from Noah's apartment that night, he didn't mention it. And if Noah felt a surge of satisfaction every time Yu-won shivered in the office after that... well, that was just professional concern for proper workplace attire.)

They had rules, after all:

1. No staying over
2. No visible marks
3. No jealousy
4. No feelings

Simple. Professional. Clean.

Except nothing about this was simple or clean, and they both knew it.

It showed in the little things - how Yu-won's breath would catch whenever Noah said his name in that particular tone, low and full of promise. How Noah's jaw would tighten every time Yu-won wore one of Jaehyun's sweaters to work. How neither of them could quite meet each other's eyes in morning meetings, too aware of what had happened in that same conference room the night before.

The office noticed, of course. It was hard not to, with the way tension crackled between them during presentations. The way Yu-won's sharp comebacks had softened into something almost fond. The way Noah's criticism, while still cutting, now carried an undercurrent of something else entirely.

"They're definitely fucking," Minjung declared over coffee one morning, watching Yu-won practically sprint from Noah's office, his hair suspiciously disheveled.

"Language!" Sunhee gasped, but she was grinning. "Besides, what about Jaehyun-ssi? Did you see how he keeps bringing Yu-won coffee?"

The office betting pool had doubled, then tripled. Team Noah versus Team Jaehyun became the hottest topic in break room gossip. Nobody seemed to notice that the real competition wasn't between Noah and Jaehyun at all - it was between Noah and himself, between what he wanted and what he'd allow himself to have.

And Yu-won? He told himself he was fine with the arrangement. That he didn't want more. That his heart didn't skip whenever Noah looked at him with that intensity that made him feel like the only person in the room.

They were both very good at lying to themselves.

Until they weren't.

The first crack in their carefully constructed facade appeared on a Tuesday, exactly three weeks into their "arrangement." Yu-won had fallen asleep on Noah's office couch, exhausted from a day of client meetings and an evening of... other activities.

He looked younger in sleep, Noah thought, watching him from the doorway. Softer. The sharp edges of his wit dulled by exhaustion, leaving just... Yu-won. Not the marketing prodigy or the office flirt or whatever other labels people put on him.

Just Yu-won.

Noah's chest tightened at the sight.

He should wake him. Should remind him of rule number one - no staying over. Should maintain the professional distance they'd agreed on.

Instead, he found himself draping his jacket over Yu-won's sleeping form, careful not to wake him.

It didn't mean anything, he told himself. It was just... courtesy. Professional concern for a valued employee.

But as he watched Yu-won burrow into the warmth of his jacket, making a small sound of contentment, Noah realized they might have a problem.

Because this? This felt dangerously close to breaking rule number four.

And that was the one rule they couldn't afford to break.
