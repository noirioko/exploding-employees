It happens during another company dinner, of all places. Yu-won is picking at his food, half-listening to the conversations around him, when Noah's voice cuts through the noise.

"Yu-won-ssi," Noah says, his tone deceptively light as he pours another round of soju. "Perhaps you should stick to water. We wouldn't want a repeat of... last time. When you get drunk, you tend to kiss just anyone, don't you?"

The words hit like a slap. Yu-won's chopsticks freeze halfway to his mouth as the meaning sinks in. Last time. Their first kiss. The one Yu-won had saved for twenty-six years, dreaming of the perfect moment, the perfect person.

The one Noah thinks meant nothing.

"I don't-" Yu-won starts, but his voice catches. How did they get here? How did his perfect romance turn into... this?

A month ago, he was just Yu Yu-won, the marketing manager with an embarrassingly obvious crush on the cold Executive Director. He'd imagined their first kiss would be romantic - maybe after a successful project, or during a late-night presentation when the office was empty and the city lights made everything feel magical.

Instead, he got drunk and clumsy and desperate, and Noah thought... Noah assumed...

Yu-won stands abruptly, his chair scraping against the floor. "Excuse me," he mumbles, fleeing to the restaurant's bathroom.

The fluorescent lights are harsh as he stares at his reflection. Who is this person? This Yu-won who runs to Noah's office at every beckoning, who lets himself be touched and marked and claimed like he's done this a thousand times before? How did the sweet romance he'd imagined turn into hurried encounters in locked offices and bitter implications about his virtue?

"You okay?" Jaehyun's voice makes him jump. His hyung leans against the bathroom door, concern evident in his expression. "You looked pale back there."

Yu-won tries to laugh, but it comes out hollow. "Just... the alcohol."

"Funny," Jaehyun says softly. "You haven't touched your drink all night."

The kindness in his voice almost breaks Yu-won. Because how can he explain? How can he tell anyone that the great office romance everyone's betting on started with a misunderstanding? That Noah Fischer, the man everyone thinks he's sleeping around with, was actually his first everything?

God, his first. Not just his first kiss, but his first... everything. And Noah thinks he does this with everyone. Thinks Yu-won is experienced enough to have other "arrangements," to need rules about exclusivity like he's some playboy collecting conquests.

If only Noah knew. Yu-won has been exclusively his from the start. Has only ever been his.

"You could tell him, you know," Jaehyun says, as if reading his thoughts. "About... that night. About it being your first."

Yu-won's head snaps up. "How can you be so calm about this? After I told you that morning..."

"Yu-won-ah," Jaehyun's smile is gentle but sad. "Of course I remember what you told me in the break room. Besides, I've known you for years. You used to blush when people held hands in dramas, remember?"

A laugh bubbles up, slightly hysterical. "And now look at me. Sneaking around with the Executive Director like some-"

"Like someone in love?"

The word hits harder than Noah's implications ever could. Love. Is that what this is? This ache in his chest whenever Noah looks at him like he's just another conquest? This desperate need to keep their arrangement going, even as it slowly kills him?

"He thinks I kiss random men when I'm drunk," Yu-won whispers, the truth finally spilling out. "He thinks that night... our first kiss... meant nothing. That I probably do it all the time."

"And you haven't corrected him because...?"

"Because-" Yu-won's voice cracks. "Because what if he likes that version better? The experienced Yu-won who knows what he's doing? Not some... some virgin who saved himself for someone who thinks he's just an office sl-"

"Yu-won." Jaehyun cuts him off sharply. "Is that really what you think? That Noah Fischer, who glares at anyone who touches you, who rearranged the entire project system just to keep you close, would prefer some imaginary version of you?"

"You don't understand," Yu-won says, pressing the heels of his hands against his eyes. "The way he looked at me that morning... like I was just another notch in his belt. And now he keeps making these comments about me being drunk and easy, like that night meant nothing, like my first kiss was just some... some drunken mistake-"

His voice finally breaks completely, tears spilling over. Jaehyun pulls him into a hug, letting Yu-won cry against his shoulder.

"Oh, Yu-won-ah," Jaehyun sighs, rubbing his back. "When did you get so tangled up in this mess?"

Yu-won doesn't answer. Because the truth is, he's been tangled up in Noah Fischer since the moment they met. He just never imagined their story would turn out like this - all backwards and twisted, full of assumptions and pride and secrets.

His phone buzzes in his pocket. Another message from Noah: 'Where are you?'

Yu-won stares at the screen, tears still falling. How can three simple words feel like both a lifeline and a noose?

And how much longer can he keep pretending this arrangement isn't slowly breaking his heart?
