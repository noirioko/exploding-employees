The marketing department doesn't know what to make of Yu Yu-won lately.

He's back to being that bright, chaotic force of nature they remember from a month ago - showing up five minutes late to meetings with coffee and a dramatic story about subway delays, charming clients with his quick wit and infectious laugh, wearing those ridiculous sneakers with his expensive suits like fashion rules don't apply to him.

It's like the past month of tension never happened. Like he didn't spend weeks disappearing into Noah Fischer's office at odd hours. Like his smile reaches his eyes again.

Except it doesn't. Not really.

"And then," Yu-won tells the creative team, gesturing wildly with his iced americano, "the client's daughter shows me her TikTok account, right? Two million followers! We absolutely have to-"

His voice cuts off as Noah walks past the conference room. Just for a second - so brief most people miss it. But Jaehyun catches the way Yu-won's hands tighten on his cup, how his laugh comes just a beat too late.

"Yah, Yu-won-ah," Jaehyun calls out, drawing attention away from that subtle crack in Yu-won's performance. "You're getting coffee all over the mock-ups!"

"Aish, hyung!" Yu-won's pout is picture-perfect as he dabs at the papers. "Why didn't you stop me sooner?"

"Because you're cute when you're excited~"

From his office, Noah's pen snaps in half.

The scene repeats throughout the day - Yu-won being deliberately, aggressively FINE. He jokes with the interns, helps the design team brainstorm, even starts planning the company dinner with his usual flair for the dramatic.

"Noraebang is non-negotiable," he declares, perched on Jaehyun's desk while reviewing venues. "The creative department needs to show off!"

"You just want to sing that new NewJeans song," Jaehyun teases, reaching up to ruffle Yu-won's hair.

Yu-won doesn't flinch away from the touch like he has been all month. Instead, he leans into it, his smile bright but brittle. "Hyung knows me so well~"

The sound of a laptop slamming shut echoes from Noah's office.

When Yu-won finally packs up for the day, his carefully maintained energy starts to crack. His shoulders slump just slightly as he gathers his things, exhausted from pretending to be that carefree Yu-won from before... before everything.

"Ready to go?" Jaehyun appears at his desk, coat in hand. "I'm craving army stew."

"Mm, sounds good." Yu-won's smile is smaller now, but more genuine. "Let me just-"

"Yu-won-ssi."

They both freeze at Noah's voice. Yu-won doesn't turn around, but Jaehyun sees how his hands shake as he organizes his desk.

"The Anderson revisions," Noah continues, his tone perfectly professional. "They're due tomorrow."

"Ah, right." Yu-won's voice is light, practiced. He still doesn't turn. "I'll have them on your desk first thing, Director Fischer."

The formal title lands like a slap. Noah's jaw tightens, but his voice stays steady. "See that you do."

As Noah's footsteps fade, Yu-won finally lets out a shaky breath. Jaehyun pretends not to notice how glassy his eyes look.

"Come on," Jaehyun says softly, wrapping an arm around Yu-won's shoulders. "Let's get you fed."

Yu-won leans into the touch, too tired to maintain his bright facade. "Thanks, hyung."

They're almost to the elevator when Noah emerges from his office again. Yu-won feels his gaze burning into his back but forces himself to keep walking. When the elevator doors open, he finally turns - not because he wants to, but because he knows it'll hurt more if he acts like Noah isn't even worth acknowledging.

His eyes meet Noah's for just a moment, and he lets his bright mask slip just enough to show how annoyed he is. Then he steps into the elevator, letting Jaehyun's arm around his waist guide him inside.

The last thing Noah sees is Yu-won's perfectly practiced smile as he laughs at something Jaehyun whispers in his ear.

    ---
    

The army stew is good, but Yu-won keeps checking his phone anyway. Not because he's waiting for anything. Definitely not because he half-expected Noah to text him some excuse about urgent revisions.

But his phone stays silent.

"You know," Jaehyun says after their third bottle of soju, "we haven't done noraebang in ages. Want to go? Just us?"

Yu-won hesitates, then nods. Maybe singing will help drown out the silence of his phone. "Yeah. Yeah, let's go."

The noraebang booth is small and cozy, fairy lights casting everything in a soft glow. Yu-won scrolls through the song list, determinedly NOT picking any of the love songs he's been avoiding all month.

"IU?" Jaehyun suggests, but Yu-won shakes his head.

"Too sad. I want... I want..." He keeps scrolling until he finds it. "Big Bang! Something loud!"

Three songs in, Yu-won's tie is loose and his jacket is abandoned on the couch. He belts out the chorus of BANG BANG BANG like his life depends on it, and maybe it does. Maybe if he screams loud enough, he'll stop feeling like this.

"Yu-won-ah." Jaehyun's voice is gentle when the song ends. "You don't have to pretend with me."

"Pretend what?" Yu-won reaches for another song. "I'm having fun! This is fun!"

"Is it? Because you keep checking your phone like-"

"I'm not checking anything!" Yu-won's voice cracks. "I'm not... I'm not waiting for..."

He drops onto the couch, suddenly exhausted. The microphone falls from his hand, rolling across the floor.

"Why am I like this?" he whispers. "Why do I keep hoping he'll... when he clearly doesn't..."

Jaehyun sits beside him, pulling Yu-won's head onto his shoulder. "Because you love him."

"I don't want to anymore." Yu-won's voice is small. "It hurts too much."

"I know." Jaehyun strokes his hair. "I know it does."

They sit in silence for a moment, the karaoke machine cycling through its demo songs. Then Yu-won sits up, wiping his eyes.

"One more song?" he asks, trying to smile. "Something really stupid this time?"

"Only if you promise to hit the high note in Gee."

Yu-won laughs - a real laugh this time, even if it's a little watery. "Deal."

His phone stays dark all night. He pretends it doesn't matter.

The neon lights paint shadows across Yu-won's face as he scrolls through the song list again. His fingers hover over a ballad - one of those sad love songs he's been avoiding - when Jaehyun catches his hand.

"Yu-won-ah." Jaehyun's voice is soft, different. "Look at me."

Yu-won turns, confused by the shift in tone. In the dim light, Jaehyun's eyes are serious as they search his face.

"What if..." Jaehyun's thumb brushes over Yu-won's knuckles. "What if I could help you forget him?"

"Hyung?"

"Just for tonight." Jaehyun leans closer, and Yu-won should move away, should remember why this is a bad idea, but... but Noah hasn't texted, has he? Hasn't even tried to stop him from leaving. Maybe... maybe...

When Jaehyun's lips meet his, Yu-won doesn't pull away. The kiss is gentle, careful - everything Noah's kisses aren't. It should feel right. Should feel safe.

It feels wrong.

But Yu-won kisses back anyway, tears slipping down his cheeks as he tries desperately to feel something, anything other than the ache in his chest that only Noah seems to cause.
