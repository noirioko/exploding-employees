"Is this still just an arrangement?"

Noah's eyes darken as they search Yu-won's face. "Do you want it to be?"

The question hangs heavy between them. Yu-won's fingers tighten in Noah's shirt, Jaehyun's words echoing in his mind: 'Some hearts are worth protecting.'

He just isn't sure which ones anymore.

"Yu-won." Noah's voice is low, urgent. "Answer the question."

But Yu-won can't. Because saying no means admitting this is more than just convenience, more than just stress relief. And saying yes...

Well, that would be the biggest lie of all.
