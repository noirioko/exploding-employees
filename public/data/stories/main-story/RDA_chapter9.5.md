Yu-won barely makes it to his desk the next morning when Noah's voice cuts through the office like ice:

"My office. Now."

The entire marketing department pretends to be very interested in their screens, but Yu-won knows they're all watching. His cheeks burn as he remembers Minjung from accounting's not-so-subtle whispers about seeing him enter the Paradise Hotel with Jaehyun last night.

The moment Yu-won closes Noah's office door, he finds himself slammed against it.

"I did NOT agree to revoke rule number six," Noah growls, his hands gripping Yu-won's waist hard enough to bruise. "What part of exclusive didn't you understand?"

"Let go of me-"

"Did you let him touch you?" Noah's voice drops dangerously low. "Did you let him see you like I have?"

Yu-won's breath catches, memories of last night flooding back - how even Jaehyun's gentlest touches felt wrong, how his body refused to respond no matter how much he wanted it to. But Noah doesn't deserve to know that. Not when he's acting like this.

"What if I did?" Yu-won lifts his chin defiantly. "You don't own me, Director Fischer. I can do whatever I want with whoever I-"

Noah's mouth crashes into his, brutal and possessive. For a moment, Yu-won's traitorous body melts into it - because THIS is what he's been craving, what Jaehyun's sweet kisses couldn't replicate.

But then he remembers why he's angry, why he's hurting, and he shoves Noah away.

"Stop it!" Yu-won's voice shakes. "You can't just... you can't keep doing this! Making me feel like I'm nothing and then getting angry when someone else wants me!"

"Nothing happened," Noah says flatly, but his eyes are dangerous. "Tell me nothing happened."

"Why?" Yu-won laughs bitterly. "Would you believe me even if I said that? You've already decided what kind of person I am, haven't you?"

Noah's jaw tightens. "Yu-won-"

"For the record," Yu-won cuts him off, his voice cracking, "Jaehyun-hyung was a perfect gentleman. Sweet. Caring. Everything you're not."

He doesn't add that that's exactly why it felt so wrong. Why his body wouldn't - couldn't - respond to anyone but Noah.

"Then why aren't you with him right now?" Noah asks quietly, something dark flickering in his eyes.

The question hits harder than any accusation. Because they both know the answer, don't they? Because no matter how much Yu-won wishes he could want the nice guy, his stupid heart and treacherous body only want Noah.

"I have work to do," Yu-won says instead of answering. "The Anderson presentation-"

"You're not leaving until you tell me nothing happened."

"Or what?" Yu-won's smile is sharp but his eyes are wet. "You'll punish me? Add more rules to our arrangement? Face it, Noah-ssi. You can't control who I sleep with anymore."

The lie tastes bitter on his tongue. Because they both know he couldn't sleep with anyone else if he tried. His body made that perfectly clear last night.

But Noah doesn't need to know that. Doesn't deserve to know how pathetically loyal Yu-won's heart - and body - are to him.

Let him think the worst. It's what he's always done anyway.

"Yu-won-ssi," Noah's voice turns dangerous, silky. "Do you really want to play this game?"

"Game?" Yu-won laughs, but it comes out shaky. "Is that what this is to you? Just another power play?"

"You know exactly what this is." Noah steps closer, and Yu-won hates how his body automatically leans toward the heat of him. "And you know the rules."

"The rules?" Something in Yu-won snaps. "Like how you're allowed to treat me however you want, but I can't even have dinner with my best friend? Like how you can make comments about me being drunk and easy, but I'm supposed to just take it? Those rules?"

"Yu-won-"

"No, let's talk about the rules." Yu-won's voice rises, weeks of hurt spilling out. "Let's talk about how you get to act like I'm nothing but convenient, but the moment someone else shows interest-"

"Someone else?" Noah's hands slam against the door on either side of Yu-won's head, caging him in. "You mean Jaehyun? The one who took you to a hotel last night?"

Yu-won flinches. Not because Noah's angry - he expected that. But because for just a second, underneath the rage, there's something that looks almost like pain in Noah's eyes.

But that's impossible. Noah Fischer doesn't feel pain over things like this. Doesn't feel anything beyond possession and pride.

"What Jaehyun and I do is none of your business," Yu-won says, proud that his voice barely shakes. "You made that very clear when you-"

His words cut off in a gasp as Noah's mouth finds that spot behind his ear - the one that always makes Yu-won's knees weak. His body betrays him instantly, arching into the touch like it's been starving for it.

"None of my business?" Noah murmurs against his skin. "Then why are you trembling, Yu-won-ssi? Why does your body react like this when I touch you?"

"Stop-" Yu-won tries to push him away, but his hands fist in Noah's shirt instead. "You can't just..."

"Can't what?" Noah pulls back just enough to meet his eyes. "Can't remind you who you belong to?"

"I don't belong to anyone!" Yu-won's voice cracks. "I'm not... I'm not just something you own. You can't keep making me feel like this and then act like I mean nothing-"

He freezes, realizing he's said too much. Noah goes very still, his eyes searching Yu-won's face.

"Make you feel like what?"

Yu-won turns his face away, tears threatening again. "Just... let me go. Please."

But Noah's hand catches his chin, forcing him to meet that intense gaze. "Like what, Yu-won?"

The way Noah says his name - soft, almost gentle - is what breaks him. Because this is exactly what he can't handle. These moments when Noah acts like he might actually care, right before he goes back to treating Yu-won like a convenient body to warm his bed.

"Like I could be special," Yu-won whispers, the truth finally spilling out. "Like I could be more than just another hole for you to-"

A knock at the door makes them both jump.

"Director Fischer?" Minjung's voice carries through the wood. "The Samsung team is here for their 10 AM."

Right. The Samsung meeting. Not Yu-won's presentation - that's the Anderson one at 2 PM. The one he still needs to fix because Noah kept demanding revisions yesterday before... before everything happened.

Yu-won uses Noah's momentary distraction to slip away, straightening his clothes with shaking hands.

"I'll be ready for the Anderson presentation at 2," he says, his professional mask sliding back into place even as tears threaten. "Director."

He doesn't look back as he opens the door, doesn't see the way Noah's hands clench at his sides, doesn't hear the soft curse that escapes when he's gone.

He has four hours to pull himself together. Four hours to practice pretending his heart isn't breaking. Four hours to prepare for standing in front of Noah and their biggest client while acting like everything is fine.

He can do this. He has to.
