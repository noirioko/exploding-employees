# Axis Rules Don't Apply
## Chapter 4: Office Politics

### The Walk of Shame

The marketing department's break room buzzes with poorly concealed whispers.

"Same clothes as yesterday," Minjung from digital hisses over her coffee. "And did you see his neck?"

"Director Fischer kept him here all night for 'revisions'," Sunhee adds, making air quotes. "But Jaehyun-ssi brought him a fresh shirt this morning! The DRAMA!"

"Speaking of Jaehyun-ssi, what was with those finger hearts in the meeting? So bold!"

"Not as bold as Director Fischer basically announcing they'd be working 'all night' in front of everyone!" 

"Shh! They're coming!"

Yu-won drags himself into the office at half-past ten, hair still damp from his shower, collar pulled high despite the warm weather. His entire body aches, reminding him of everything he's trying to forget. He slips toward his desk, trying to be invisible in yesterday's wrinkled clothes.

A paper bag appears in front of him.

"Thought you might need this." Jaehyun's voice is gentle but his eyes are sad. "There's a fresh shirt and some coffee. Hyung will always take care of you."

From his office, Noah watches Yu-won accept Jaehyun's kindness while wearing the evidence of their night together. Something dark and possessive twists in his chest.

### The Break Room

"I can't." Yu-won's hands are firm on Jaehyun's chest, pushing him back even as tears threaten to fall again. "Hyung, I just... I can't."

Jaehyun feels his heart crack at the rejection, but keeps his voice gentle. "Because of him? Noah?"

Yu-won stiffens. "How did you-"

"You really think I haven't noticed?" Jaehyun's laugh is soft, masking years of watching Yu-won pine for someone else. "The way you look at him? How hard you try to impress him? And now these marks..." His fingers brush Yu-won's collar, jealousy burning in his chest at each dark reminder of what happened. "Did he hurt you?"

"No! I mean... not like that." Yu-won slumps against the counter, all his energy draining. "It was my fault. I got drunk and stupid and... and now he probably thinks I'm..."

"Thinks you're what?"

Yu-won's laugh comes out broken. "Easy? Experienced? A slut who does this with everyone?" The words taste bitter. "He didn't even believe it was my first..."

"Wait." Jaehyun's eyes widen, something painful twisting in his chest. All this time he'd been holding back, thinking Yu-won was too experienced for someone like him, and now... "Your FIRST?"

Yu-won covers his face with his hands. "I didn't tell him! I tried but... but he kept talking about you, kept assuming things, and I just... I let him think what he wanted."

"Yu-won-ah..." Jaehyun pulls him into a hug, all earlier tension forgotten. "Why didn't you tell me?"

"Because it's humiliating!" Yu-won's voice cracks. "I saved myself for so long, hoping... and now he thinks I do this with everyone. He probably thinks last night meant nothing."

"Then tell him the truth."

"What's the point?" Yu-won pulls away, wiping his eyes. "He made it clear what he thinks of me. Called me annoying, told me to get out... God, I can't even look at him now-"

As Yu-won finishes his confession, Jaehyun makes a decision. He's done watching from the sidelines.

"Yu-won-ah." His voice makes Yu-won pause. "I'm not giving up on you."

Yu-won turns slowly, confusion clear on his face. "Jae-hyung...? What are you..."

The break room door opens. They both freeze.

Noah stands there, expression carved from ice. "If you're done with your personal drama, the Anderson team is waiting."

Yu-won straightens immediately, professional mask sliding into place despite his red eyes. "Of course, Director Fischer. I'll be right there."

Noah's jaw tightens at the formal title. "Now, Yu-won-ssi."

As Yu-won hurries past him, Noah catches a glimpse of fresh marks on his neck - right where Jaehyun's mouth had been. Something dark twists in his chest.

"Quite efficient," he says coldly, loud enough for Yu-won to hear. "Moving on so quickly."

Yu-won stumbles but doesn't turn around. His shoulders shake with suppressed tears as he practically runs to the meeting room.

Jaehyun steps forward, eyes blazing. "You don't deserve him."

Noah's smile is sharp enough to cut. "Funny. That's exactly what I was thinking about you."

### The Post-It

Yu-won changes in the bathroom, hands trembling as he buttons up Jaehyun's shirt. It fits perfectly - of course it does. Jaehyun knows his size, knows how to take care of him, knows everything about him.

Unlike someone else who just keeps hurting him.

When Yu-won returns to his desk, there's a post-it note waiting:
'The layouts still need work. My office. Lunch.'

Yu-won crumples the note, Jaehyun's shirt suddenly feeling like armor against whatever fresh hell awaits him.
