# Rules Don't Apply
## Chapter 4.5: The Arrangement

They lie in Noah's office bed, carefully not touching despite what just happened. The silence weighs heavy with unspoken words and stubborn pride. Yu-won stares at the ceiling, trying to ignore how his body still tingles with aftershocks, how it responded to Noah's touch like it was made for him. So much for pretending he preferred Jaehyun's gentleness - his traitor body knew exactly what it wanted, who it wanted. Not that he'll ever admit it.

"So." Noah's voice breaks the silence, carefully neutral. "About this arrangement."

"What arrangement?" Yu-won says petulantly, pulling the sheet higher even though it's pointless now. "This was a one-time thing."

Noah props himself up on one elbow, studying Yu-won's profile. Something doesn't add up - the way Yu-won had trembled under his touch, those little gasps of surprise at every new sensation... but then there's the confident way he talks about Jaehyun, how easily he takes Noah's roughness...

"Your body suggests otherwise." He keeps his tone light, testing. "Unless Jaehyun-ssi really is gentler?"

Yu-won's flush deepens, but his voice stays sharp. "Maybe I just like variety."

The words hit their mark - Noah's imagination fills with images of Yu-won with Jaehyun, with others, and something possessive twists in his chest. But then he remembers Yu-won's expression of shocked pleasure, like every touch was a new discovery...

No. He's reading too much into this.

"If we're doing this," Yu-won continues, still not looking at him, "there need to be rules."

"Rules?" Noah pushes aside his doubts. This is just physical - no need to overthink it.

"No staying over."

"No marks where people can see."

"No... getting jealous when I'm with others."

That last rule makes Noah's jaw clench, but Yu-won's challenging smirk stops him from arguing. He's playing right into whatever game this is.

"Fine." Noah holds out his hand like this is just another business deal. "Just sex. No feelings, no complications."

Yu-won takes it, and Noah definitely imagines the slight tremor in his fingers. "Deal."

Neither mentions how their hands linger too long, how their bodies gravitate toward each other even now. That would be too much like feelings. And feelings aren't part of the arrangement.

Even if Noah can't shake the nagging feeling that he's missing something important. Even if part of him wants to grab Yu-won and demand the truth about his experience, about Jaehyun, about everything. But that would mean admitting he cares. And caring isn't part of the arrangement either.
