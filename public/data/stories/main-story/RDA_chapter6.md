Yu-won stares at his reflection in the office bathroom mirror, fingers absently tracing the fading mark on his collarbone. Was he really the same person who, just weeks ago, had never been touched? The same Yu Yu-won who blushed at hand-holding and saved his first everything for someone special?

Now here he is, running to Noah's office at the slightest hint of a summons, letting himself be bent over expensive desks and pressed against window panes high above Seoul's skyline. He's become something else entirely - someone who knows exactly how to arch his back to make Noah lose control, someone who's memorized which buttons of Noah's shirts pop off easiest.

"What are you doing to yourself?" he whispers to his reflection.

The worst part is, he didn't even directly agree to come when Noah demanded his presence last night. He'd been at dinner with Jaehyun, determined to prove he could have a life outside whatever this thing with Noah was. And yet...

One text. That's all it took.

'My office. Now.'

And like a puppet on strings, he'd made his excuses and gone running. Not because Noah ordered it - he tells himself this firmly - but because... because...

He can't even finish that thought.

"No more dinner dates," Noah had said after kissing him senseless. Like he had any right to make demands about Yu-won's personal life.

The memory makes something rebellious spark in Yu-won's chest. He straightens his tie, that familiar sharp edge returning to his smile.

When he walks into Noah's office that afternoon, there's a deliberate sway to his hips.

"You wanted to see me, Director Fischer?"

Noah looks up from his laptop, his eyes darkening as they track Yu-won's movement. "Close the door."

Yu-won does, but he doesn't move closer. Instead, he leans against it, head tilted in false innocence. "I thought you didn't care who I had dinner with. Isn't that what you said? That it's not part of our... arrangement?"

Noah's jaw tightens. "Yu-won-"

"I mean, if you don't care about dinner..." Yu-won examines his nails, feigning nonchalance even as his heart races. "I guess you don't care who else I-"

He doesn't finish. In three swift steps, Noah has him pressed against the door, one hand gripping Yu-won's waist hard enough to bruise.

"Careful," Noah growls, his breath hot against Yu-won's ear. "You're playing a dangerous game."

"Am I?" Yu-won's voice wavers slightly, but he keeps pushing. "Because last I checked, this was just an arrangement. Just convenient stress relief between two consenting adults. So really, what does it matter if I find other... arrangements?"

Noah's grip tightens impossibly further. "Is that what you want? To let anyone else touch you? To let them see you like this?"

"You didn't seem to mind the idea before," Yu-won shoots back, remembering all too well how Noah had assumed he was experienced that first night. "What was it you thought? That I did this with everyone? That I was just some office sl-"

Noah kisses him then, hard and bruising, swallowing the bitter words. When he pulls back, his eyes are dark with something dangerous.

"New rule," he says, voice rough. "This becomes exclusive. I don't share, Yu-won-ssi, and I won't fuck someone who's being touched by god knows who else."

Yu-won's breath catches, his heart doing something complicated in his chest. But his pride makes him ask: "And if I don't agree?"

Noah's smile is sharp enough to cut. "Then the arrangement ends. Your choice."

Yu-won swallows hard, his body already betraying him. The truth burns in his throat - he couldn't end this if he tried. His body knows Noah's touch too well now, craves it with an intensity that terrifies him. Even the faint trace of Noah's cologne makes his knees weak, his mind flooding with memories of heated nights and desperate touches.

Every cell in his body has memorized Noah - the weight of his hands, the heat of his mouth, the way he can take Yu-won apart with just a look. The thought of never feeling that again...

"Fine," he breathes out, the word catching in his throat. "Exclusive. Whatever."

Noah's answering kiss is possessive enough to make Yu-won's world spin. He kisses back just as desperately, knowing he's already in too deep but unable to stop himself from drowning.
