# Chapter 4 (continued): The One Who Made Me Feel Like This

The slap echoes in the quiet office. Yu-won's hand stings, tears finally spilling over.

"Don't." His voice shakes. "Don't you dare finish that sentence when you're the one who- who-"

Noah catches his wrist before he can slap him again, grip tight enough to bruise. "The one who what?"

"The one who made me feel like this!" Yu-won tries to yank free but Noah's hold is iron. "You called me annoying! You told me to get out! You used me and threw me away like I was nothing!"

"Used you?" Noah's laugh is bitter as he pulls Yu-won closer, backing him toward the private rest room attached to his office. "You're the one who ran straight to him-"

"I DIDN'T RUN TO ANYONE!" Yu-won shoves at his chest but Noah doesn't budge. "He was just being KIND! Something you clearly don't understand!"

Noah slams the door behind them, pressing Yu-won against it. One hand on either side of his head, caging him in. "Kind? Is that what you call it when he marks what's mine?"

"YOURS?" Yu-won's voice cracks, half-laugh, half-sob. "I'm not YOURS! You made that perfectly clear when you-"

"When I what?" Noah leans in closer, so close Yu-won can feel his breath. "When I fucked you? When I made you scream my name? When I-"

"When you called me ANNOYING!" The word rips out of Yu-won like a confession. "When you looked at me like you regretted everything! When you-"

They're both breathing hard now, faces inches apart. Yu-won's tears streak down his cheeks but his eyes are blazing with fury and hurt and something desperate.

"You don't get it," Yu-won whispers, voice breaking. "That was my first time. My FIRST. And you-"

Noah goes completely still. "What?"

"And you treated me like I was just another-" Yu-won's voice catches. "Like I meant nothing-"

Noah's mouth crashes against his, swallowing the rest of that sentence. It's not gentle - it's desperate and angry and claiming. Yu-won makes a broken sound against his lips, fighting and melting at the same time.

"You're lying," Noah growls between kisses, but his hands are already sliding into Yu-won's hair, tilting his head for better access. "You can't have been-"

"Why would I LIE about that?!" Yu-won gasps when Noah's mouth moves to his neck. "You're the one who ASSUMED-"

Noah pulls back just enough to stare at him, something dark and possessive and almost scared flickering in his eyes. "First?"

Yu-won's laugh is wet and bitter. "Congratulations, Director Fischer. You ruined something I can never get back."

The words hit like another slap. Noah's jaw clenches, his grip on Yu-won tightening. "Yu-won-"

"What?" Yu-won's tears fall faster now. "What could you possibly say? That you're sorry? That you didn't mean to make me feel worthless? That-"

Noah kisses him again, harder this time. Desperate. Like he's trying to erase every cruel word, every assumption, every moment of misunderstanding with the pressure of his mouth.

And Yu-won... Yu-won kisses back just as desperately. Because his body knows this, craves this, even when his heart is breaking.

"I hate you," Yu-won whispers against Noah's lips, even as his hands clutch at Noah's shirt. "I hate that you made me feel like this-"

"Then hate me properly," Noah growls, lifting Yu-won off his feet. Three steps and he's throwing Yu-won onto the small bed in the corner of the rest room - the one meant for overnight work sessions but never actually used for sleeping.

Yu-won bounces once, eyes wide and wet as Noah looms over him. "What are you-"

"You want to hate me?" Noah's hands are already working on Yu-won's buttons, rough and impatient. "Then hate me while I prove you're MINE. That you've always been mine. That your body knows exactly who it belongs to-"

"You don't get to-" But Yu-won's protest dies as Noah's mouth finds that spot behind his ear, the one that makes him arch helplessly.

"Watch me," Noah breathes against his skin, and Yu-won realizes with a desperate, furious clarity that he's already lost.

Because even now, even hurt and angry and crying, his body responds to Noah's touch like it's the only thing that matters.

Noah strips them both with ruthless efficiency, hands rough where they usually take time to tease. There's no gentleness here - just desperate, angry need.

"I hate you," Yu-won gasps as Noah's fingers work him open, faster and less careful than before. "I hate that I can't stop wanting-"

"Good." Noah's voice is rough as he positions himself. "Hate me all you want. But you're still mine."

When Noah pushes in, it's hard and claiming and nothing like their first time. Yu-won cries out - from pain, from pleasure, from the overwhelming feeling of being filled by the only person who can make him feel like this.

"Say it," Noah demands, his rhythm brutal and perfect. "Say you're mine."

"Never-" Yu-won's denial breaks into a moan as Noah hits that spot inside him. "I won't-"

Noah's hand wraps around Yu-won, stroking in time with his thrusts. "Say it."

"I hate you," Yu-won sobs instead, his body betraying him as he rocks back into Noah's movements. "I hate that you're the only one who can make me feel-"

"Mine," Noah growls against his ear, and it's not a question anymore.

When Yu-won comes apart, it's with Noah's name on his lips and tears streaming down his face. Noah follows seconds later, burying himself deep as he shudders through his release.

For a long moment, they just breathe. Noah's weight pressing Yu-won into the mattress, both of them sticky and spent and still angry.

Yu-won's voice is small when he finally speaks. "I really do hate you, you know."

Noah's laugh is hollow as he pulls out, reaching for tissues to clean them both. "I know."

"This doesn't change anything."

"I know that too."

They lie there in silence, carefully not touching despite what just happened. The anger has burned down to ashes, leaving only exhaustion and hurt and the terrible awareness that they're both in way too deep.

"So." Noah's voice breaks the silence, carefully neutral. "About this arrangement."

And Yu-won realizes with crushing certainty that this is how it's going to be. Anger and desperate sex and pretending feelings don't exist.

He should leave. Should have some self-respect.

But when Noah starts listing rules, Yu-won finds himself agreeing. Because having Noah like this - angry, possessive, complicated - is still better than not having him at all.

Even if it's destroying them both.
