The rain hits Yu-won's face as he bursts out of the restaurant, mixing with tears he can't seem to stop. When did he become this person? This crying, messy version of himself who can't seem to hold anything together anymore?

He used to be different. Used to be Yu Yu-won, the marketing genius with the sharp tongue and bright laugh. The one who made clients smile, who could charm anyone with a well-timed joke. He never cried. Never let anything shake his confidence.

Then Noah Fischer walked into his life and suddenly he's this - someone who runs from restaurant bathrooms with tears streaming down his face, someone who gets kissed by his best friend and feels nothing but guilt, someone who can't stop crying no matter how hard he tries.

"Yu-won!" Noah's voice cuts through the rain. "Stop running!"

Yu-won runs faster, his dress shoes slipping slightly on the wet pavement. He makes it halfway down the block before strong fingers wrap around his wrist, yanking him into a narrow side street.

"Let go!" Yu-won tries to pull away, but Noah pushes him against the brick wall, caging him in.

"Not until you explain what that was about!"

"What's there to explain?" Yu-won laughs bitterly, rain dripping from his hair. "You saw enough, didn't you? Poor easy Yu-won, can't keep his legs closed for one minute-"

"Stop it." Noah's voice cracks slightly. "Just... stop crying, Yuyu-"

Yu-won freezes. "What... what did you just call me?"

Noah looks almost embarrassed, his usual cold facade cracking. "You just... you cry a lot. Like one of those round fish. Yuyu..."

Yu-won stares at him, caught between disbelief and hysteria. Here he is, having an emotional breakdown in a rain-soaked alley, and Noah Fischer - cold, professional Noah Fischer - is comparing him to a fish?

He doesn't know whether to laugh or cry harder. Trust Noah to do this now, to show this unexpectedly dorky side when Yu-won is trying to express his inner turmoil. It reminds him painfully of why he fell for this man in the first place - these rare glimpses of the real Noah behind the icy exterior.

But no. He can't think about that now. Can't let himself be charmed by Noah's awkward attempt at... whatever this is.

"Stop it," Yu-won says, pulling away. "You can't just make jokes right now. Not after what you said in there-"

"I know." Noah's hands tighten on Yu-won's waist. "I know, and I'm... I shouldn't have said that. But seeing you kiss him-"

"He kissed ME!" Yu-won shouts, finally snapping. "I didn't kiss him back! I've never-" He stops himself, realizing he's saying too much. His voice drops to a whisper. "Just... forget it. It doesn't matter anyway."

"What doesn't matter?" Noah steps closer, rain dripping from his hair. "What were you going to say?"

Yu-won turns his face away, unable to look at Noah when he's this close, when he looks at Yu-won like he actually cares. "You've made it very clear what you think of me. What's the point?"

"Yu-won-"

"I should go." Yu-won pushes against Noah's chest, needing to escape before he says something else he can't take back. "We have that client meeting tomorrow and I need to-"

"Stop running away from me."

"Why?" Yu-won's laugh is bitter. "So you can accuse me of something else? Make more comments about how I probably kiss everyone when I'm drunk?"

Noah's jaw tightens. "That's not-"

"It's exactly what you meant." Yu-won finally meets his eyes, tears mixing with rain. "You know what? Maybe you're right. Maybe I should go find someone else to kiss. At least Jaehyun-hyung never makes me feel like shit like this."

The words land like a physical blow. Noah goes completely still, his eyes darkening dangerously. For a moment, the only sound is the rain hitting the pavement.

Yu-won can't take it back now. Can't explain that he only said it to hurt Noah the way Noah keeps hurting him. So he does what he's best at lately - he runs.
