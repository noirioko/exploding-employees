# Rules Don't Apply - Character Introduction

## MAIN CHARACTERS

### Noah Fischer (29)
**Position:** Executive Director at AXIS Creative Group

**Personality:**
The intimidating boss everyone's a little scared of - brilliant, efficient, and seemingly cold. Multilingual and devastatingly competent at his job. But beneath that icy exterior? Completely whipped for a certain chaotic junior designer. Possessive, jealous (counts touches like it's a sport), and terrible at expressing feelings. Will literally transfer people to Busan for being mean to Yu-won.

**The Catch:** Comes from generational wealth (Fischer Corp family), but started AXIS as his passion project. Pretends he's not obsessed with Yu-won. Everyone sees through it.

**Animal Vibes:** Dragon/Tiger - Overprotective, territorial, hoards his precious goldfish

---

### Yu Yu-won (26)  
**Position:** Junior Designer (New hire - 3 months at company!)

**Personality:**
The office sunshine baby who became everyone's favorite in record time. Extroverted, loud laugh that echoes through floors, wears old sneakers to work, eats terrible convenience store food (makes Noah cringe). Cries at EVERYTHING - happy, sad, angry, doesn't matter. Creative genius who terrifies clients with his brilliance, but also the emotional disaster who gets flustered easily. Saved himself for someone special (spoiler: it's Noah, who doesn't know this and assumes he's experienced - cue PAIN).

**The Catch:** Been crushing on Noah since day one. His "first everything" but too proud/scared to admit it. Actually has all the power in the relationship despite seeming like the soft one.

**Animal Vibes:** Goldfish/Bunny/Kitten - Small, precious, makes everyone want to protect him (especially Noah)

---

### Yu Jaehyun (28)
**Position:** Senior Account Manager/Project Lead (Founding employee)

**Personality:**
Everyone's reliable hyung with a fox-like charm. Warm, kind, remembers everyone's coffee orders. The perfect gentleman who brings clients and money to AXIS with his charisma. But beneath that? Pined for Yu-won for 3 YEARS (yes, while Yu-won was a barista at the café he frequented). Finally convinced Yu-won to join AXIS, only to watch him fall for Noah instead. Ouch. Used to be a playboy who broke hearts, now he's the one with the broken heart. Learning to be vulnerable after years of playing games.

**The Catch:** Wears multiple hats at AXIS as founding employee. Guides Yu-won's projects (professionally painful since he's in love with him). Trying to move on.

**Animal Vibes:** Capybara - Gentle giant, friend to all, secretly needs someone to take care of him too

---

### Kim Minkyu (25)
**Position:** Junior Account Manager (New hire, replaces Dongho)

**Personality:**
The ultimate tsundere but ONLY around Jaehyun. Professional, organized, tries SO hard to look competent... then Jaehyun smiles and Minkyu malfunctions completely. Drops files, spills coffee, turns into a blushing mess. Gets extra snippy when jealous (especially of Yu-won, since he sees how close Jaehyun and Yu-won are). Actually very good at his job when Jaehyun isn't around to scramble his brain. Proud on the surface, soft romantic underneath.

**The Catch:** Finds Jaehyun devastatingly handsome but doesn't realize Jaehyun is equally whipped for him. Gay panic 24/7.

**Animal Vibes:** Mink - Tiny, proud, will fight you, actually very soft

---

## COMPANY: AXIS Creative Group

**What they do:** Full-service marketing/advertising agency landing huge clients (Samsung, Anderson, LG) despite being a relatively new startup!

**Size:** 80-100 employees, 5-floor building

**The Tea:** 
- Noah's personal wealth = family money (Fischer Corp)
- AXIS is his passion project started ~3 years ago
- Jaehyun was his first employee
- Small enough that everyone knows everyone's business
- 4th floor break room = gossip headquarters
- Office betting pools on relationships are THRIVING

## THE RELATIONSHIPS

### Noah & Yu-won
**The Vibe:** Overprotective dragon hoarding his oblivious goldfish

**The Drama:** Drunk kiss → disastrous first time (SO many misunderstandings!) → painful "arrangement" with rules → jealousy & miscommunication → feelings explosion → finally admitting they're in love

**The Dynamic:** Noah thinks he's in control. Yu-won actually runs everything. Noah is SO whipped but tries to hide it. Yu-won cries constantly but is stronger than he looks. They're both disasters at communication.

---

### Jaehyun & Minkyu
**The Vibe:** Patient capybara dealing with defensive baby mink  

**The Drama:** Elevator meet-cute → Jaehyun's fox games → Minkyu's jealousy over Yu-won → "arrangement" → devastating rejection → redemption arc

**The Dynamic:** Jaehyun thinks he's playing games. Minkyu is genuinely falling. Both have to learn how to be vulnerable and honest.

---

## THE TIMELINE

**3 years ago:**
- Noah starts AXIS, Jaehyun joins
- Jaehyun discovers nearby café, meets barista Yu-won
- Jaehyun pines for 3 years (becomes regular customer)

**3 months ago:**  
- Jaehyun convinces Yu-won to join AXIS
- Yu-won starts as Junior Designer
- Noah tries not to obsess (fails immediately)

**Story begins:**
- Noah gives Anderson project to Yu-won (the newbie!)
- Dongho (former junior account manager) gets jealous
- Dongho makes nasty comment about Yu-won → transferred to Busan
- Minkyu hired to replace Dongho
- Office drama EXPLODES

## WHY THIS STORY HITS DIFFERENT

- **Miscommunication as a love language** (they're SO bad at talking!)
- **Power dynamics** (boss/employee but Yu-won has the real power)
- **Virgin/experienced assumption** (Noah assumes wrong, pain ensues)
- **Office gossip as Greek chorus** (Minjung & Sunhee see EVERYTHING)
- **Found family vibes** (the whole office becomes family)
- **Emotional vulnerability** (crying is not weakness!)
- **Second chances** (Jaehyun learning to love healthily)
