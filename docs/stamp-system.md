# Stamp System

A reward and achievement system where you collect stamps by completing tasks and reaching milestones. Think of it like achievement badges or merit stamps!

## 🎫 What Are Stamps?

Stamps are visual rewards/achievements that mark your accomplishments:
- Collected by completing specific tasks or milestones
- Displayed in a stamp collection book
- Each stamp has unique artwork
- Some stamps are rare or secret
- Purely cosmetic (no gameplay bonuses)

## 🎮 Stamp Earning Strategy

### Easy Stamps (Beginner)

Start with these:
- Complete 10 tasks (any type)
- Earn 1,000 won
- Collect first card
- Roll first gacha

### Medium Stamps (Intermediate)

Work towards:
- 30-day streak
- 50 tasks completed
- All employees S rank once
- 5 hearts with any character

### Hard Stamps (Advanced)

Long-term goals:
- 100-day streak
- All cards collected
- All AU books unlocked
- 10 hearts with all characters

### Secret Stamps (Expert)

Hidden challenges:
- Complete task at midnight
- Use specific furniture combo
- Trigger rare employee dialogue
- Easter egg discoveries

## 💡 Tips for Collectors

1. **Check stamp book regularly** - See what you're close to earning
2. **Diversify tasks** - Don't just do one type
3. **Consistency matters** - Many stamps require streaks
4. **Explore features** - Try everything to discover secret stamps
5. **Don't stress** - Stamps are fun extras, not requirements
