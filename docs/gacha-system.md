# Gacha & AU Books System

A gacha (random roll) system for collecting Alternate Universe (AU) story books about the four employees. Think of it like loot boxes, but for fanfiction!

## 📚 What Are AU Books?

AU Books are alternate universe stories featuring the four employees (Yuwon, Jaehyun, Minkyu, Noah) in different scenarios and settings.

**Types of AUs:**
- Romance AUs (office romance, coffee shop, etc.)
- Fantasy AUs (magic, supernatural)
- Historical AUs (different time periods)
- Modern AUs (different jobs, locations)
- Crack AUs (silly, absurd scenarios)

**Each AU Book Contains:**
- Title and description
- Multiple story snippets (paragraphs/scenes)
- Character interactions
- Full story arc
- Cover art/illustration

**Total AU Books:** 20 unique books to collect

## 🎰 Gacha Mechanics

### How to Roll

1. Go to **Store page** (`/store`)
2. Find the Gacha section
3. Click "Roll Gacha" button
4. Cost: **100 YuCash per roll**
5. Get a random AU book you don't own yet

### Roll Results

**Success:**
- Reveals a random AU book from uncollected books
- Book is added to your Library
- Animation shows book reveal
- Can immediately view in Library

**Duplicate Protection:**
- Can't get duplicate books
- Always get a book you don't have yet
- Guarantees completion after 20 rolls

**Cost Efficiency:**
- 20 books × 100 YuCash = 2000 YuCash total
- Guaranteed to collect all books for 2000 YuCash
- No gacha hell / infinite grinding!

### Gacha History

All your rolls are recorded:
- Date/time of roll
- Which book you got
- How much YuCash spent
- Total books collected

View history on Store page or Library page

## 📖 Reading AU Books

### Library Page (`/library`)

The Library is where you read unlocked AU books:

**Features:**
- Bookshelf display of all unlocked books
- "Locked" indicator for books you haven't rolled yet
- Click book to open reading interface
- Track reading progress per book

### Story Snippets

Each AU book has multiple snippets (story segments):

**Snippet System:**
- Each book has 5-15 snippets
- Snippets are initially **blurred/locked**
- Must spend YuCash to reveal each snippet
- Snippet costs vary: 50-500 YuCash each

**Snippet Pricing:**
- Early snippets (setup): 50-100 YuCash
- Middle snippets (plot): 100-300 YuCash
- Climax snippets (juicy): 300-500 YuCash
- Resolution snippets: 100-200 YuCash

**Why Snippet Pricing?**
- Gives you control over reading pace
- Don't have to unlock full story at once
- Most exciting scenes cost more (but worth it!)
- Adds progression beyond just collecting books

### Reading Interface

**Book View:**
- Book cover at top
- List of all snippets (blurred if locked)
- Click snippet to unlock (costs YuCash)
- Once unlocked, snippet stays revealed forever
- Read unlocked snippets in order

**Progress Tracking:**
- X / Y snippets unlocked
- Total "Juice Spent" (YuCash spent on snippets)
- Progress bar showing completion %

## 💰 Cost Breakdown

### Full Collection Cost

**To Collect All Books:**
- 20 books × 100 YuCash per roll = **2,000 YuCash**

**To Unlock All Snippets:**
- Average book: 1,500-2,500 YuCash in snippets
- 20 books × ~2,000 YuCash = **~40,000 YuCash**
- High estimate: Up to 50,000 YuCash

**Total Investment:**
- Collect all + read all = **~42,000-52,000 YuCash**
- Long-term goal (months of gameplay)
- Designed as endgame content

### Budget Strategy

**Phase 1 - Collection (2,000 YuCash):**
- Roll gacha whenever you have 100 YuCash
- Prioritize collecting all 20 books first
- Gives you overview of all available stories

**Phase 2 - Selective Reading:**
- Read snippets of your favorite books first
- Unlock cheap snippets to get story flow
- Save expensive snippets for later
- Focus on favorite characters/AUs

**Phase 3 - Completionist:**
- Unlock remaining snippets
- Complete all 20 books fully
- Achievement: "Loremaster" 📖

## 💡 Tips & Strategy

### Collecting Tips

1. **Save up 2,000 YuCash** - Can collect all books in one session if desired
2. **Roll consistently** - One roll per day keeps variety
3. **Don't stress RNG** - You'll get all books eventually
4. **Check Library after each roll** - Enjoy your new acquisition

### Reading Tips

1. **Preview all books first** - Collect all 20 before deep reading
2. **Choose favorites** - Focus YuCash on books you like most
3. **Unlock cheap snippets** - Get story context before expensive ones
4. **Read in order** - Snippets are meant to be read sequentially
5. **Budget your juice** - Don't blow all YuCash on one book

### Completionist Path

1. Week 1-2: Collect all 20 books (2,000 YuCash)
2. Month 1: Unlock snippets for 3-5 favorite books
3. Month 2-3: Continue unlocking favorites
4. Month 4+: Complete remaining books
